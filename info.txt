Atari Multi 68k

An openFPGA core for Atari Games' "Escape" arcade hardware (dual 68000):
Escape from the Planet of the Robot Monsters (E.P.R.O.M.), plus the Klax
and Guts n' Glory prototypes that ran on the same board.

Ships no ROMs. Supply your own dumps. Early scaffold — see docs/ARCHITECTURE.md.
