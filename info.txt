Atari Dual 68k (Escape from the Planet of the Robot Monsters, 1989)
Alpha release candidate - openFPGA core for Analogue Pocket

Dual 68000 + JSA-I audio (YM2151 + TMS5220 speech), hall-effect stick
emulation. High scores and operator settings persist to the SD card.
ROM required: MAME 'eprom' set, user-supplied (not included).

Boots to a clean screen. A small cyan build number sits bottom-right -
check it matches the build you installed.

L1 shows/hides the diagnostic HUD (off by default).
R1 cycles its 6 pages (0-5). L2 toggles the trace view.
With the HUD up, holding R1/L2/R2 isolates video layers - a probe, not
a fault. These do nothing while the HUD is off.
More toggles in the Interact menu (+ button).

Known imperfect: sprite dropouts in dense crowds; slight shimmer on
1-pixel diagonals (Pocket scaler, not fixable in the core); some scenes
run marginally under arcade speed. No save states.
