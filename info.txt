Atari Dual 68k (Escape from the Planet of the Robot Monsters)
Alpha - openFPGA core for Analogue Pocket

Dual 68000 + JSA-I audio (YM2151), hall-effect stick emulation.
ROM required: MAME 'eprom' set (not included).

R button cycles debug modes 0-7 (first build-ID digit).
Debug/tuning toggles in the Interact menu.

github.com/spoonelli/Atari-Dual-68k  (GPL-3.0)
Uses TG68K, T65, jt51 (see third_party licenses).
